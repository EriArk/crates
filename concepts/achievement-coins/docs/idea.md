# Original Idea — Achievement Coins

The initial idea was a playful TrainerOS-wide reward for game completion and RetroAchievements. Its use was originally undecided, with cosmetics only one possible direction.

**Superseded by the owner-approved concept of 20 October 2026:** the coins are now a **spendable, single shared cosmetic currency** earned by eligible verified play across TrainerOS and the creator's first-party games. The cosmetic store is open to all, free or supporting. Verified Patreon/Ko-fi membership increases the **earn rate up to ×3**, not a monthly package of coins; higher tiers may offer dev/beta access. Existing purchases and balances persist after ending support. No pay-to-win and no shop discounts in the current concept.

**Still not specified:** prices, earning amounts, exact tiers, RetroAchievements and game-completion proof, one-time rewards, offline transactions, trade/transfer and entitlement recovery. This remains planning documentation, not a claim of implementation.
