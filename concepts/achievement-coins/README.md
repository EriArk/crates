# Achievement Coins

**Status:** Experimental idea; the purpose of the coins is intentionally undecided. This is not a real-money currency or approved in-game economy.

## Idea

Players may receive small virtual **coins/tokens** for:

- Finishing games where completion can actually be established.
- Unlocking RetroAchievements achievements with trustworthy identity and achievement IDs.

This could create a playful layer of progress spanning different games inside TrainerOS.

## Possible uses — NOT decisions

- Cosmetic profile decoration, frames or titles.
- Cosmetic clan emblems or banners.
- Cosmetic options for TrainerOS-supported mini-games.

It may turn out that **a simple non-spendable lifetime token/achievement score is more useful than an actual spendable currency**. Do not commit to a shop, trade, cross-user transfer, scarcity, monetary value, pay-to-win perks or anything affecting competitive rankings.

## To discuss later

- What benefit does the system provide beyond existing RetroAchievements points?
- How is completion verified for games without RetroAchievements?
- One-time reward vs repeat play, and duplicate achievement avoidance.
- Account scope, privacy, recovery and offline behavior.
- Whether the idea merits a built-in feature at all.

No fabricated "game completed" detection; any award requires a supportable source or clearly user-reported progress.
