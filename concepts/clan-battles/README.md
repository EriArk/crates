# Fluxer Clans & Clan Battles

**Status:** Initial concept. Shared host/platform integration to be discussed later; not a separately approved game, module, or shipped feature.

## Idea

Let people form or designate clans using **existing Fluxer communities** and organize competitions between them. A clan should use the social identities, groups, roles and membership relationships already available rather than recreate a second chat/account system.

- Communities can challenge one another to matches or event series.
- Clan battles can reuse the [competitive mini-games](../../games/competitive-minigames/) and their common GameHub Lobby and results infrastructure.
- The same community opt-in mechanism planned for the mini-games could make a community eligible to act as a clan.
- Match results are attached to verified game/match and player IDs; clan-level standings could aggregate them if a future rule is chosen.
- Invitations, presentation of events and results, and team rosters should be available from appropriate game/community interfaces.

**Not yet decided:** whether every community can become a clan or only opt-in ones; team membership vs community membership; inter-community permissions; match formats, scoring, seasons, player switches, winner verification and moderation.

No additional chat service, mandatory backend or parallel Lobby is intended. The actual Fluxer capabilities and rights must be checked before implementation.
