# Idea: Fluxer-Integrated Competitive Mini-Games

Create a series of compact competitive multiplayer games deeply connected to Fluxer and TrainerOS.

## Games mentioned

Tetris-like falling blocks, ping-pong, tanks, racing, block-breaker, an Icy Tower-like tower-climbing game, and others.

## Social and multiplayer

- An individual community for every game.
- Automatic lobby pre-fill, when possible.
- Data exchange via Fluxer wherever practical.
- In-game access to social and multiplayer information so players do not have to repeatedly leave for the Social screen.
- A natural foundation for tournaments, ratings, rankings and other competitive features.

The actual real-time networking mechanism is still open for discussion; tight Fluxer integration does not imply routing all gameplay traffic through it.

This document records the initial concept only. Mechanics, matchmaking, technical APIs, ranking, UI, and scope remain to be designed.
