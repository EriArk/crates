# Arcade Today

**Type:** Widget for TrainerOS  
**Status:** Selected concept, not implemented.

A Dashboard card featuring a daily challenge from the competitive mini-games collection.

## Initial scope

- Show the featured challenge.
- Surface participation and result information when supported.

## Server-side results (proposal only)

A future [TrainerOS Backend](https://github.com/EriArk/TrainerOS/blob/main/docs/TRAINEROS_BACKEND_PLAN_RU.md) could verify and record the official daily challenge, results and winners, and publish limited information through Fluxer. The challenge design, scheduling, prizes (e.g. crowns/trophies/coins), anti-cheat and reward rules have **not** been finalized. This widget must not independently invent or grant authoritative awards.

## Integration notes

Reuse the shared mini-game/community framework; daily scheduling, fairness and result validation remain to discuss.
