# Music Service Widgets

A collection of lightweight music player widgets for TrainerOS.

The idea is to have **a separate widget for each music service**, rather than one universal client. These are small, focused players, not full replacements for the services' official apps.

## Basic features

- Shuffle and play the user's liked tracks.
- Choose and play an existing playlist.
- Keep playback controls simple and easy to access from TrainerOS.

## Organization

Each service will have its own project folder and documentation, once the target services are chosen.

**Status:** Concept only. Individual services and implementation details will be decided later.
